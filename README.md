## 🌟 Key Features

- 🗺️ **Interactive Radar Map**: Real-time virtual roaming, GPS controls, wild spawns, Pokéstops, and Raid Gyms.
- 🎯 **Pokéball Throwing Physics**: Drag, swipe, aim, and curveball with shrinking capture rings (*Nice, Great, Excellent!*).
- 🐾 **40+ Iconic Pokémon**: Pikachu, Charizard, Blastoise, Gengar, Mewtwo, Rayquaza, Lucario, and more with high-res sprites.
- 🏛️ **Pokéstops & Item Drops**: Spin interactive photodises to earn Pokéballs, Great Balls, Ultra Balls, and Razz Berries.
- ⚔️ **Gym Raid Battles**: Real-time battle arena with Fast Attacks, Charge Moves, Dodges, and Raid Boss encounters.
- 📖 **Pokédex & Storage**: Power up with Stardust & Candies, track caught/seen Pokémon, and evolve your favorites.
- 🔊 **Web Audio Synthesizer**: Built-in 8-bit sound effects for throws, captures, level-ups, and gym battles.
- 💾 **Auto-Save**: Automatic persistent progress saving via browser `localStorage`.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Move Your Trainer**: Use the on-screen DPAD, `W/A/S/D`, or Arrow keys, or click **Auto Roam**.
2. **Catch Pokémon**: Tap on any wild Pokémon on the radar map to start an encounter. Swipe the Pokéball towards the Pokémon to throw!
3. **Spin Pokéstops**: Tap a nearby Pokéstop and spin the photo disc to collect free items and XP.
4. **Raid Gyms**: Enter the Gym Raid Arena to battle legendary Raid Bosses!
